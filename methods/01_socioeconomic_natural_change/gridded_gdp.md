# Estimate annual gridded GDP per capita

**English** | [中文](gridded_gdp_ZH.md)

Predict current-price CNY per person with county observations and a fixed feature order. Five land-class shares use valid classified pixels as denominator. Night light per capita uses total light and same-year population within the support area. The remaining features are year, latitude and longitude. Use the frozen historical county panel and verified centroids, harmonize administrative keys and train a 4,403-round gradient-boosted tree on log positive observed GDP per capita. Exponentiate the prediction.

For paired validation, both holdout fits exclude all years of the same counties. The historical holdout baseline uses the historical eligibility rule. For yearly forward validation use the original trained baseline and fit the candidate on prior observed years only. Compare the same pre-calibration samples, reporting log RMSE, log R², and median absolute percentage error by year and pooled. When the original gate is enabled, require lower RMSE and median APE in both pooled validation domains before a final fit.

Fill missing county observations with predictions. First adjust the population-weighted county mean to the same-year provincial target. Then adjust the population-weighted 0.01° predictions to the calibrated county values. Weight the coefficients by county coverage in crossing cells. Verify provincial closure and report county readback error from the actual GeoTIFF. County-fit metrics, grid closure and independent pixel accuracy answer separate questions. Independent grid-level truth is unavailable in the handoff.

The subsequent update adds same-year 2021–2024 predictors and observed county targets, retrains on 2000–2024 observations and adds paired county/forward-year validation. These additions must be described separately from the original paper. Historical 2000–2020 grids retain their earlier model generation; the extended series is not a uniform 2000–2024 recomputation. This AirMosaic task tests synthetic execution and does not independently revalidate those formal project results.

The proposed Zenodo record groups historical and updated years in one versioned dataset, explicitly recording model generations and each year's actual extent. A possible public package contains GDP_pc-only grids and a six-column county table, omitting population and calibration factors. Projection and packaging require exact readback against originals. Upstream CSMAR terms require redistribution authorization; permission for derived tables, weights and grids remains to be established. No public package or upload is performed by this workflow.
