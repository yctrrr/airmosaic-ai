# Literature evidence and synthesis

**English** | [中文](literature_ZH.md)

## Research question

Define what claim or decision the literature should inform. A bounded topic survey, a systematic review and reproduction of a particular study need different search and inclusion designs; choose the design before interpreting the collected papers.

## Sources and scope

Record search sources, query strings, dates, language/time limits and inclusion/exclusion rules. Prefer original papers and official supplements for methodological claims. Distinguish preprints, accepted manuscripts, published versions and corrections. A paper's existence or accessibility does not establish access to its data, code or complete model specification.

## Organization and synthesis

Deduplicate by DOI and normalized bibliographic identity while preserving version relationships. Link main text, supplements, data and code as distinct evidence objects. Extract a structured record of question, sample, measurements, design, assumptions, comparison, results and limitations, with page/section references. Separate authors' conclusions from the reviewer's inference. Compare studies on equivalent populations, outcomes, units and study designs before combining estimates.

## Error and quality

Assess selection/publication bias, source coverage, missing attachments, reporting completeness and study-specific validity threats. Search snippets and generated summaries are discovery aids rather than substitutes for the relevant full-text passage. A methodological detail absent from accessible material remains unresolved. Keep inclusion/exclusion decisions and conflicting results visible; do not infer consensus from the number of downloaded files.

## Interpretation

State what the evidence supports, for which population and under which assumptions. Distinguish an association, a predictive result and a causally identified effect. Literature synthesis motivates hypotheses and model choices; it does not validate a new dataset or reproduce a computation by itself.
