The maintained PAF formula, rate units, age-sex denominator rules, counterfactual exposure and uncertainty interpretation live in the [health method](../../../../../methods/04_integrated_impacts/health_impacts.md). Use [operations](operations.md) for the actual template assumptions and adaptation checks.

Historical bibliography retained from this skill:

- Burnett R, Chen H, Szyszkowicz M, et al. (2018). Global estimates of mortality associated with long-term exposure to outdoor fine particulate matter. *PNAS*, 115(38), 9592–9597.
- Burnett RT, Pope CA III, et al. (2014). An integrated risk function for estimating the Global Burden of Disease attributable to ambient fine particulate matter exposure. *EHP*, 122(4), 397–403.
- GBD 2019 Risk Factors Collaborators (2020). Global burden of 87 risk factors in 204 countries and territories, 1990–2019. *The Lancet*, 396(10258), 1223–1249.
- Historical note: “Burnett RT, Spadaro JV, et al. (2022). An updated global exposure mortality model for fine particulate matter. Draft manuscript.” This incomplete draft reference is unverified; it must not establish a curve version or authorize numerical assumptions.

The previous generic zero-TMREL, age-invariant IER and illustrative RR-value comparisons are not valid universal defaults. Select the exact published curve table, endpoints and age eligibility for each task. Actual ER source cells and published log-linear coefficients have now been acquired separately; see [source records](exposure_response.md). No complete GEMM/IER curve integration or real health computation has been validated.

Newer guidance: [WHO HRAPIE-2 2025](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789289062633), Table 2. Exact versions and applicability are maintained in the ER source record, not inferred from this bibliography.
